Inkbrawl privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Inkbrawl is a drawing and battle game provided by Bosqa. Contact: mirzhan.urazalin@gmail.com.

Playing and saving

Inkbrawl does not require a game account and has no free-text chat. Drawings, saved arenas, game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Android may include eligible app data in backup and restore features according to your device settings. Clearing app data removes local saves; uninstalling may remove them as well. Files you export are saved to a location you choose and remain under your control.

Advertising

The native app uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads between some completed fights and optional rewarded ads. Ink Club members do not receive ad requests from the game's ad integration. The browser version does not request AdMob ads.

Ad requests are configured as child-directed, under the age of consent, and General content rated. These settings restrict ad personalisation but do not mean the advertising SDK collects no data. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Processing depends on Google's SDK and the applicable settings. AdMob's SDK uses encrypted connections for this processing.

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform provides privacy messages and consent choices before ads are requested. When Google requires a privacy options entry point, the Ink shop displays an Ad privacy choices button to revisit those choices. The Android app removes the advertising ID permission; other SDK processing described above can still apply. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases and Ink Club

Optional cosmetic packs and Ink Club subscriptions are processed through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. The store processes payment and associated purchase information. Inkbrawl does not receive card or bank details. The game receives product identifiers, purchase status and store transaction data needed to unlock, acknowledge and restore purchases.

Ink Club renews monthly or yearly, depending on the selected plan, until cancelled in the store account. Cancellation normally leaves access through the end of the paid period. The store displays the current price, billing period and applicable terms before purchase. Purchase actions use a grown-up question before opening the store's purchase flow; this question is not identity or age verification.

Children

Children are part of Inkbrawl's intended audience. The game does not ask players to enter a real name, address, phone number or email. Advertising and purchase processing by the platform providers still apply as described above. Parents can control purchases and device use through the platform's family settings. Contact us with privacy concerns at the email below.

Online features

The current Android package has no public game server configured, so online rooms, the world leaderboard and shared arenas are unavailable. Local drawings and arenas are not uploaded by those features in this package.

If a future release enables a server, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated before that release. The web game's optional online features can transmit a random device identifier, generated nickname, scores, selected match drawings and arenas that a player chooses to publish. This Android policy does not describe an independently hosted web server's processing.

Retention and choices

You can remove local saves by clearing app data and delete exported files using your device's file controls. Store purchase records and advertising-provider records follow the providers' retention policies. We cannot delete Google's or Apple's records on their behalf. For questions or requests concerning information controlled by Bosqa, contact mirzhan.urazalin@gmail.com.

Contact and changes

Support and privacy requests: mirzhan.urazalin@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be reflected in the date above and, where required, in the app and store disclosures.
