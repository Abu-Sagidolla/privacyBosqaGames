Inkbound privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Inkbound is a drawing and battle game provided by Bosqa. Contact: mirzhan.urazalin@gmail.com. Inkbound is suitable for players aged 9 and older, including children, and is designed so that it does not need personal information from any player.

What the game stores on your device

Inkbound does not require an account, does not ask for a name, email address, phone number or address, and has no free-text chat. Your drawings, saved arenas, game progress, Ink Drops balance, boosts and settings are stored on your device. Android may include app data in backup and restore according to your device settings. Clearing app data removes these saves. Files you export are saved where you choose and stay under your control.

Advertising

The mobile app uses Google AdMob to show ads between some completed fights and optional rewarded ads. Ink Club members do not receive ads. The browser version does not request AdMob ads.

All ad requests are marked as child-directed and as being for users under the age of consent, with General content rating. This means no personalised advertising and no interest-based targeting. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may still process technical data such as IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device information, app and ad interactions and diagnostic information, for serving ads, measuring them and preventing fraud. We only use advertising networks that are certified for children's apps under Google Play's Families Policy. The Android app does not use the advertising ID permission. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform shows privacy messages, and the Ink shop has an "Ad privacy options" button to review those choices. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Optional packs, Ink Drops and Ink Club subscriptions are processed by Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. The store handles payment. Inkbound does not receive card or bank details. The game receives product identifiers, purchase status and transaction data needed to unlock, restore and acknowledge purchases. Before opening a store purchase, the game asks a grown-up question; this is not identity or age verification, so parents should also use their store's parental controls. Ink Club renews monthly or yearly until you cancel it in your store account; you keep access until the end of the paid period. Ink Drops and boosts are virtual items for use inside the game and have no cash value.

Online features

When online play is available, the game connects to the Inkbound game server to run duels. This can send the drawing you selected for a match, your in-match actions, a random identifier created by the game (not linked to your name or device identity) and, if you take part in the leaderboard, a generated nickname and score. Nicknames are generated by the game, not typed by players. Shared arenas are only uploaded if you choose to publish them. There is no chat and no voice or free-text messaging; players can only use a few fixed emotes. Match data is kept only as long as needed to run the match and the leaderboard.

Children

Inkbound can be played by children aged 9 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game does not ask for it. We do not use children's data for advertising profiles. Parents and guardians can contact us at the email below to ask questions or to request removal of any data we may hold. Parents can control purchases and device use through their platform's family settings.

Your choices and retention

You can remove local data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Store purchase records and advertising-provider records follow those providers' retention policies, and we cannot delete Google's or Apple's records on their behalf. To ask about information controlled by Bosqa, contact mirzhan.urazalin@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes, and update the app and store disclosures where required.
