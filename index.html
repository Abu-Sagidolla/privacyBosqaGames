Inkbound privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Inkbound is a drawing and battle game provided by Bosqa. Contact: mirzhan.urazalin@gmail.com. Inkbound is intended for players aged 13 and older.

What the game stores on your device

Inkbound does not require an account and has no free-text chat. Your drawings, saved arenas, game progress, Ink Drops balance, boosts and settings are stored on your device. Android may include app data in backup and restore according to your device settings. Clearing app data removes these saves. Files you export are saved where you choose and stay under your control.

Advertising

The mobile app uses Google AdMob to show ads between some completed fights and optional rewarded ads. Ink Club members do not receive ads. The browser version does not request AdMob ads.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Ad requests are configured with restricted settings (no personalised advertising), and the Android app does not use the advertising ID permission. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform shows privacy messages and consent choices, and the Ink shop has an "Ad privacy options" button to change them. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Optional packs, Ink Drops and Ink Club subscriptions are processed by Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. The store handles payment. Inkbound does not receive your card or bank details. The game receives product identifiers, purchase status and transaction data needed to unlock, restore and acknowledge purchases. Ink Club renews monthly or yearly until you cancel it in your store account; you keep access until the end of the paid period. Ink Drops and boosts are virtual items for use inside the game and have no cash value.

Online features

When online play is available, the game connects to the Inkbound game server to run duels. This can send the drawing you selected for a match, your in-match actions, a random device identifier, and a generated nickname and score if you take part in the leaderboard. Shared arenas are only uploaded if you choose to publish them. There is no chat. Match data is kept only as long as needed to run the match and the leaderboard.

Children

Inkbound is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game does not ask for a real name, address, phone number or email. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices and retention

You can remove local data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Store purchase records and advertising-provider records follow those providers' retention policies, and we cannot delete Google's or Apple's records on their behalf. To ask about information controlled by Bosqa, contact mirzhan.urazalin@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes, and update the app and store disclosures where required.
